💡 Project Overview: AgriMarket
Challenge Title:
Digital Markets for Local Farmers
________________________________________
Challenge Statement:
How might we design platforms or systems that connect rural farmers directly with urban consumers, restaurants, or retailers — enabling fair pricing, reduced waste, and transparent food sourcing?
________________________________________
Background & Context:
In South Africa, many small-scale rural farmers depend on informal markets with unstable pricing and limited buyer access. As a result, they face low, inconsistent income and significant post-harvest losses due to unsold produce.
At the same time, urban buyers (e.g., consumers, restaurants, schools) struggle to find fresh, affordable, and traceable local produce. This gap creates inefficiencies in the food system and limits economic opportunities for farmers.
Digital platforms can bridge this divide by allowing direct connections between farmers and buyers, removing middlemen, improving transparency, and strengthening rural economies.
________________________________________
✅ Our Solution
We are building a simple, mobile-friendly web platform that connects local farmers directly with urban consumers or restaurants. It will serve as an online marketplace that enables farmers to list their produce, set fair prices, and receive orders directly from buyers.

The platform will also feature:
•	Real-time messaging between farmers and buyers in case of interest
•	Mobile payment options (via wallet or payment proof)
•	AI-powered assistant (via Gemini API) to help with:
o	Price suggestions
o	Best-selling crop recommendations
o	Quick buyer/farmer support
________________________________________
🧱 Core Features
1. Farmer Dashboard
•	Register/Login
•	Upload product info (name, image, price, stock)
•	View and manage orders
•	Chat with buyers
•	Badge rate (based on past buyers rating products or farmer)
2. Buyer Dashboard/home website
•	Browse listed farm products
•	Search & filter by product/category
•	Place orders
•	View order history
•	Chat with farmers
3. AI Assistant (Gemini-API)
•	Suggest fair prices based on market conditions
•	Recommend which crops to plant based on season
•	Guide users on how to use the platform
4. Admin Panel (Optional for system support but important for users validation)
•	Approve farmer registrations
•	View total orders, product stats
•	Manage flagged users or listings
5. Payment System
•	Simple proof-of-payment (upload receipt) or mobile wallet option (e.g., M-Pesa, PayFast)
•	Track order payment status
